FAQ

Simple answers to common questions.

Who are these services for?

Our services are designed primarily for small and growing organisations that need practical IT, cybersecurity and governance support.

Do you provide 24/7 SOC monitoring?

No. Our current focus is assessment, documentation, technical support and security improvement services rather than a 24/7 managed SOC.

Can you perform vulnerability assessments?

Yes, where we have explicit authorisation and an agreed scope. Assessments are documented with findings, severity and remediation recommendations.

Do you certify organisations against ISO 27001?

No. We provide ISO 27001 readiness and gap assessments to help organisations understand their current position and prepare for formal certification activities.
