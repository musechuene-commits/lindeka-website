FAQ

Simple answers to common questions.

Who are these services for?

Our services are primarily suited to small and growing organisations that need capable IT, security and governance support without building large internal teams.

Do you offer ongoing monthly IT support?

Yes. We provide structured monthly remote IT support retainers for growing organizations that need priority helpdesk availability without the cost of a full-time internal IT team.

Do you provide 24/7 SOC monitoring?

No. We currently focus on assessments, documentation, technical support and security improvement rather than operating a 24/7 managed SOC.

Can you perform vulnerability assessments?

Yes, with explicit authorisation and an agreed scope. Each assessment records findings, severity and recommended remediation.

Do you certify organisations against ISO 27001?

No. We provide ISO 27001 readiness and gap assessments to show where an organisation stands and what should be addressed before formal certification activities.
